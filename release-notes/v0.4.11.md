## What's Changed

### Performance Optimization Suite (SQLite/FTS5, Async Concurrency, TUI Cache & Pixel Pipeline)
- **SQLite/FTS5 & Async Pipeline**: Migrated `onenote_fts` to `rowid`-indexed deletes (`O(N²) → O(log N)`), wired `prepare_cached` across 60 storage statements, offloaded blocking storage calls in `serve.rs`/`sync.rs` to `spawn_blocking`, and replaced chunked `join_all` HoL barriers in `graph/onenote.rs` with bounded `buffer_unordered` streams.
- **Parser & TUI**: Promoted `Selector::parse` to `LazyLock`, rewrote `unescape_basic_xml_entities` to single-pass `Cow`, eliminated per-frame deep clones of `OnenotePageDetail` and `RenderedTerminalImage` with a generation-keyed `CanvasRenderCache`, fixed 2-pass `html_preview` cache thrashing, and deferred `viewport_pixel_payload` in `tui.rs`.
- **Pixel Engine**: Upgraded Sixel quantization to flat 32K histogram + 5-5-5 LUT + 2-row `i16` dithering + fast ASCII RLE, switched PNG encoding to `Fast`/`NoFilter` (`fdeflate`), and enabled `opt-level = 3` for codec/pixel crates.
